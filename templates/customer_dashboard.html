🌱 Why Recycle?

Proper waste recycling helps reduce pollution, conserve natural resources and keep our environment clean. Kabadiwala Connect helps connect users with waste collectors and supports responsible recycling.

📄 Paper

Separate newspapers, books and cardboard from wet waste.

♻️ Plastic

Keep recyclable plastic clean and separate different materials when possible.

🔩 Metal

Separate metal cans and scrap materials for proper recycling.

💻 E-Waste

Do not burn or dispose of electronic waste with normal household waste.
